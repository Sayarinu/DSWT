DSWT

Wonder Trade for Nintendo DSi and 3DS with TWiLight Menu++.

Your browser creates private random seeds for your SD card. DSWT uses one when you set up a new save, so you can skip the touchscreen movements. Each download gets fresh seeds, generated on this device.

Install on your SD card

  1. Extract the ZIP. Copy its roms and _nds folders to the root of your SD card, merging them with the existing folders.
  2. In TWiLight Menu++, open roms/nds/dswt.nds in DSi mode. Choose your game to trade from its active SD save or select Game Card to view the inserted cartridge’s party.

One kit covers 32 new saves. Keep your kit private. Share this installer so each player gets their own kit.
